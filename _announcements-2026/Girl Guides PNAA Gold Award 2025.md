<p>We are proud to celebrate the achievements of our Peicai Secondary School
Girl Guide unit attaining the prestigious Puan Noor Aisha (PNAA) Gold Award
in the Year of Assessment 2025.</p>
<p>The PNAA Gold Award recognises Girl Guides who have demonstrated commitment,
leadership, resilience and active participation in Guiding. Through their
journey, our Girl Guides have taken on challenges, developed essential
life skills, served others and contributed meaningfully to their school
and community.</p>
<p>This achievement reflects their dedication, perseverance and growth throughout
their Guiding journey. We congratulate our Girl Guides on this significant
milestone and commend them for their hard work and commitment to the values
of leadership, service and friendship.</p>
