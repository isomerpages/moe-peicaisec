<p>As Artificial Intelligence (AI) continues to reshape the way we live,
learn and work, how can we prepare our students not just to use AI, but
to thrive in a future transformed by it?
<br>
<br>On 12 August 2026, Peicai Secondary School welcomed <strong>Mr. Alan Lee, Co-founder of Astria Consulting Pte Ltd</strong>,
to share with our students and staff on <strong>“Beyond ChatGPT: How AI Is Changing the Way the World Works.”</strong>
</p>
<p>Drawing on his experiences in technology, consulting, business transformation
and entrepreneurship, Mr. Lee offered students a glimpse into how AI is
already changing workplaces and the skills that will become increasingly
important in an AI-enabled world.</p>
<p>Beyond learning how to use AI tools, the session invited students to consider
what it means to remain distinctly human in the age of AI — to <strong>ask better questions, exercise sound judgement, think critically, take responsibility for decisions, and remain curious and adaptable learners</strong>.</p>
<p>&nbsp;Through this sharing, we broadened our students’ perspectives on
the future of work and encourage them to see AI not simply as a tool that
provides answers, but as one that can challenge their thinking and help
them learn, create and contribute more meaningfully.</p>
