<p>Peicai Concert Band has been awarded a 5-Star Distinction at the PAssionArts
Festival Open Stage Band Competition 2026, held on 18 July 2026 at Our
Tampines Hub. The band was one of eight participating schools that came
together to showcase their musical talents, passion and dedication to concert
band music.</p>
<p>The PAssionArts Festival is a community arts platform that brings together
artists, arts groups and the community to celebrate the arts and foster
greater appreciation for the arts. The Open Stage Band Competition provides
an opportunity for school and community bands to perform before an audience
and be assessed by experienced music professionals, while encouraging a
spirit of learning, collaboration and excellence through music.</p>
<p>The performances were adjudicated by Brando Tan, CEO of Music Elements,
and Benjamin Yeo, Singaporean composer. Their professional expertise and
feedback provided valuable opportunities for the participating bands to
learn and grow in their musical journey.</p>
<p>Representing Peicai Secondary School, the Peicai Concert Band performed
two pieces: <em>Fate of the Gods</em> by Steven Reineke and <em>Stars of Tomorrow</em> by
Kahchun Wong. Through their performance, the students demonstrated their
musical growth, teamwork and commitment to excellence.</p>
<p>This achievement is a testament to the students' dedication, perseverance
and commitment throughout their preparation. It also reflects the guidance
and support of our teachers and instructors, whose encouragement and expertise
have played an important role in the band's musical journey.</p>
<p>Congratulations to the Peicai Concert Band on this outstanding achievement!
We are proud of our students for their hard work, resilience and passion,
and for continuing to make a difference through music.</p>
