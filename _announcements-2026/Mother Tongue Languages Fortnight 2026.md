<p><strong>Mother Tongue Languages MT Fortnight 2026</strong>
</p>
<p>From 24<sup>th</sup> March to 2<sup>nd</sup> April, Secondary 1 to 3 students
participated in a range of engaging cultural activities during Mother Tongue
Languages Fortnight.</p>
<p>Designed and conducted in-house by our Chinese and Malay Language teachers,
the programmes allowed students to appreciate their Mother Tongue languages
and cultural heritage through hands-on learning.
<br><strong>Discovering Culture Through Art: </strong>Secondary 1 students
explored <strong>Chinese Face Mask Painting</strong> and <strong>Malay Batik Painting.</strong> Through
these activities, students exercised creativity and critical thinking as
they made thoughtful choices about colours, symbols and designs.
<br>
</p>
<p><strong>Bringing Traditional Stories to Life:</strong> Secondary 2 students
experienced traditional shadow puppetry through <strong>皮影戏</strong> and <strong>Wayang Kulit</strong>.
Students worked collaboratively to coordinate puppet movements, narration
and performances. In doing so, they strengthened their communication, teamwork
and adaptive-thinking skills.</p>
<p><strong>Connecting Mother Tongue with Digital Media: </strong>Secondary
3 students applied their language skills creatively through <strong>Themed Vlogging</strong>.
Through the process of planning, filming and reviewing their videos, students
developed collaboration, communication, creativity and digital literacy.</p>
<p>Through these activities, students developed important <strong>Emerging 21st Century Competencies</strong>,
including creativity, communication, collaboration, critical thinking and
digital literacy. The programmes provided a meaningful and memorable way
for students to experience, create and share the Mother Tongue language
and culture.</p>
