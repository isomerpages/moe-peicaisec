<p><u>Teacher’s Day 2026</u>
</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>We warmly welcome former students who wish to return to Peicai Secondary
School to visit their teachers on Thursday, 3 September 2026.</p>
<p>Former students may arrive from 10.30 a.m. onwards. Before coming to school,
please complete the visitor registration form at: <a href="https://go.gov.sg/visitors-registration-peicai" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">https://go.gov.sg/visitors-registration-peicai</a>
</p>
<p>Upon arrival, please sign in at the Guard Post and proceed to the canteen
to wait. You may proceed to the 2nd storey, outside the Staff Room, after
the school’s Teacher’s Day programme has ended.</p>
<p>Please note that the school will close at 12.30 p.m., and all visitors
are requested to leave the school premises by then.</p>
<p>As you will be returning to the school as visitors, please ensure that
you are appropriately attired and observe the school’s visitor guidelines
at all times. This helps us maintain a safe and respectful environment
for everyone.</p>
<p>We seek your understanding that entry may be declined if visitors are
not appropriately attired or do not comply with the school’s visitor guidelines.</p>
<p>We look forward to welcoming you back to Peicai and hope you will enjoy
the opportunity to reconnect with your teachers. See you on 3 September!</p>
