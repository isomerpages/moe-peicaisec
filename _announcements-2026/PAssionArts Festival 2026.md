<p><strong>Where Art Meets Community: Peicai Art Club at PAssionArts Festival 2026</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<p>On Saturday, 8 August 2026, our Art Club members participated in the PAssionArts
Festival (PAF) 2026 at Tampines Hub. Organised by the People's Association
Arts &amp; Culture Division and its network of Community Arts and Culture
Clubs (CACCs), the festival provides residents with opportunities to participate
in creative activities and strengthen their sense of community and belonging.
This year's theme, 'Our Neighbourhood Gems', celebrates the unique stories,
people and places that make each community special.</p>

<p>Our Art Club members played an active role by guiding members of the public
across five engaging art activities, namely stamp printmaking, marble painting,
creative upcycling using recycled milk cartons, pebble painting, and an
art painting installation, in collaboration with St Hilda's Primary School
Art Club. In doing so, they had the opportunity to develop and hone their
competencies in communication and collaboration, while adapting creatively
and taking initiative in community engagement through their interactions
with a diverse range of participants, from toddlers to the elderly.</p>
