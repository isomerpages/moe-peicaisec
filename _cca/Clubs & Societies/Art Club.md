<h4><strong>Goals &amp; Objectives</strong></h4>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Art Club offers students with interest in visual arts a safe and conducive
space to come together and hone their artistic skills through meaningful
Art projects and competitions.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>We aim to ensure members get to explore new Art techniques, expand their
knowledge in art and cultivate their spirit of creativity, innovation,
and experimentation through Art.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To develop member's imagination and creativity through visual representation.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To provide a conducive environment to explore and express their ideas
through Art.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To develop members’ ability to express experiences and ideas in their
creation.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<h4><strong>CCA Achievements</strong></h4>
<p><strong>Where Art Meets Community: Peicai Art Club at PAssionArts Festival 2026</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<p>On Saturday, 8 August 2026, our Art Club members participated in the PAssionArts
Festival (PAF) 2026 at Tampines Hub. Organised by the People's Association
Arts &amp; Culture Division and its network of Community Arts and Culture
Clubs (CACCs), the festival provides residents with opportunities to participate
in creative activities and strengthen their sense of community and belonging.
This year's theme, 'Our Neighbourhood Gems', celebrates the unique stories,
people and places that make each community special.</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 50%;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Art festival img 1" src="/images/Announcement/Art_festival_img_1.jpg">
</div>
<p>Our Art Club students came together to create, connect and celebrate through
art.</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 50%;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Art festival img 2" src="/images/Announcement/Art_festival_img_2.jpg">

<p>Our Art Club members played an active role by guiding members of the public
across five engaging art activities, namely stamp printmaking, marble painting,
creative upcycling using recycled milk cartons, pebble painting, and an
art painting installation, in collaboration with St Hilda's Primary School
Art Club. In doing so, they had the opportunity to develop and hone their
competencies in communication and collaboration, while adapting creatively
and taking initiative in community engagement through their interactions
with a diverse range of participants, from toddlers to the elderly.</p>

<p><strong>Art Club CCA – Singapore Youth Festival 2026 Arts Presentation</strong>
</p>
<p>We are thrilled to announce that our Art Club CCA has achieved a remarkable
milestone at the Singapore Youth Festival (SYF) 2026 Arts Presentation.
This year, SYF commemorates its 60th anniversary under the inspiring theme <em>'SYF 60: The ArtsXPerience - Inspire, Connect, Empower'</em>,
a celebration that recognises students' artistic growth and the creative
processes that foster innovation, build meaningful connections, and empower
young people to make a positive impact through the arts.
<br>
<br>The 2026 exhibition theme, <em>'Artist and XPerience'</em>, invited students
to reflect on their personal artistic journeys - &nbsp;the breakthrough
moments, happy accidents, and hard-won discoveries that shape each artist's
evolving creative voice. It is a theme that speaks to the heart of what
art-making truly is, not merely the final piece on display, but the entire
process of exploration, experimentation, and self-discovery that leads
to it.
<br>
<br>We are proud to share that in this highly competitive showcase, four of
our Art Club members rose to the occasion and produced works of exceptional
quality and depth. Their artworks have each been awarded the Certificate
of Recognition and selected for the on-site exhibition, which is a testament
to their dedication, creativity, and growth as young artists.
<br>
<br>Their works will be displayed at the National Gallery Singapore from 4
to 19 July 2026. This is a wonderful opportunity to witness the talent
and passion that our Art Club members have poured into their creative journeys.
We warmly invite all Peicaians to visit the exhibition and show your support
for your fellow schoolmates. Let us celebrate their achievements together
and be inspired by the stories their art has to tell.</p>
