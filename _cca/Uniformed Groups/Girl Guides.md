<h4><strong>Goals &amp; Objectives</strong></h4>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>The motto of Girl Guides is “<strong>Be Prepared</strong>”. We aim to
prepare girls for life through providing opportunities to discover and
develop their strengths and skills to the fullest potential.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>We empower our girls to plan CCA activities catered to their needs and
interests and to execute initiatives on societal issues that they care
about. Our girls have reached out to the community around us, making a
positive difference in our society.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>We hope that through Girl Guides, we will be able to:</p>
</li>
</ol>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Provide opportunities to help the girls discover and develop their strengths
and skills to the fullest</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Develop girls to become responsible citizens of the world</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Nurture the girls to be prepared for each opportunity or challenge they
may face</p>
</li>
</ul>

<h4><strong>CCA Achievements</strong></h4>
<p>Puan Noor Aisha Gold Award – Peicai Girl Guides unit</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="(PNAA) Gold Award" src="/images/Announcement/PNAA__Gold_Award.jpg">
</div>
<p>We are proud to celebrate the achievements of our Peicai Secondary School
Girl Guide unit attaining the prestigious Puan Noor Aisha (PNAA) Gold Award
in the Year of Assessment 2025.</p>
<p>The PNAA Gold Award recognises Girl Guides who have demonstrated commitment,
leadership, resilience and active participation in Guiding. Through their
journey, our Girl Guides have taken on challenges, developed essential
life skills, served others and contributed meaningfully to their school
and community.</p>
<p>This achievement reflects their dedication, perseverance and growth throughout
their Guiding journey. We congratulate our Girl Guides on this significant
milestone and commend them for their hard work and commitment to the values
of leadership, service and friendship.</p>
<p>Girl Guides Puan Noor Aishah Award – Distinction since 2013</p>
